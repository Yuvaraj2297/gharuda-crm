Terms of Service & Data Policy
1. Data Ownership & Storage

All CRM records, customer metadata, contact interactions, and document attachments belong solely to the licensee organization. Gharuda does not access, analyze, or share your data with third parties.

2. SLA Commitments

Gharuda CRM provides 99.9% uptime for core database transactions and reporting engines. Scheduled maintenance windows will be communicated 72 hours in advance.

3. Data Retention Policy

Upon subscription cancellation, data is retained for 90 days in read-only archive before permanent deletion. Export your data anytime from the admin panel.
